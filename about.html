Transforming Lives Through Mobility

Sun Orthotics and Prosthetics Centre, approved by the Rehabilitation Council of India, treats over 3,500+ clients annually across India. Our mission is to enhance lives by restoring mobility through high-quality orthotic and prosthetic care.

Our Mission

To provide affordable, high-quality orthotic and prosthetic solutions that restore independence and dignity. We are committed to breaking financial barriers, ensuring every individual, regardless of economic background, gains access to life-changing mobility devices.

Our Vision

A world where no person is limited by the lack of mobility. Through innovation, compassionate care, and our NGO partner REHAB TRUST, we aim to be India’s most trusted centre for orthotics, prosthetics, and rehabilitative excellence.

NGO Initiative

REHAB TRUST – Free Prosthetic Limb Program

Financial hardships should never prevent anyone from walking again. Through REHAB TRUST, we offer completely free prosthetic limbs to eligible economically backward clients. Our free program includes custom fitting, training, and lifelong follow-up support.

  • 100% free for qualifying individuals
  • Income certificate & medical assessment required
  • Supported by donations & CSR partnerships

Our Core Services

Patient-first approach with advanced clinical expertise

Custom Prosthetics

Orthotic Bracing

Spinal, knee, foot, and hand orthoses to correct deformities and support weak muscles.

Diabetic Footcare

Paediatric Care
